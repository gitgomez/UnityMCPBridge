# Community conduct for UnityMCPBridge

UnityMCPBridge is a small, personally maintained technical project. Discussion
does not need to be formal, but it must remain safe, relevant, and respectful.
Participation in this repository means accepting the boundaries below.

## Work with people, not against them

- Critique code, behavior, documentation, and decisions. Do not attack the
  person behind them.
- Be direct without being hostile. Explain what is wrong, provide evidence when
  possible, and leave room for correction.
- Respect differences in experience, language, identity, background, and
  working style.
- Give proper credit. Do not present another contributor's or upstream
  project's work as your own.
- Protect private information, credentials, unpublished reports, and security
  details.

Harassment, threats, hate speech, sexualized conduct, deliberate humiliation,
doxxing, impersonation, spam, and sustained disruption are not accepted.

## Respect the project's limits

An issue or pull request is an offer to collaborate, not a claim on the
maintainer's time. Repeated demands for deadlines, private support, feature
implementation, or release decisions may be closed as disruptive even when
the underlying technical request is reasonable.

This fork is independent of
[CoplayDev/unity-mcp](https://github.com/CoplayDev/unity-mcp). Do not pressure
or blame CoplayDev, its contributors, Unity Technologies, OpenAI, or other tool
vendors for decisions made in this repository. Likewise, do not use this
repository to campaign against another project or community.

## Where this applies

These expectations cover issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, commit
messages, and other channels explicitly designated for UnityMCPBridge. They
also apply when someone presents themselves as speaking for this project.

## Reporting a conduct problem

Do not publish a sensitive conduct report in a public issue. Use the
[private repository reporting channel](https://github.com/gitgomez/UnityMCPBridge/security/advisories/new)
with the title **Conduct report** and include only the information needed to
understand the incident.

That channel is also used for security reports, so label the subject clearly.
Reports are handled privately when the maintainer is available; submission does
not create a guaranteed investigation or response deadline.

## Moderation

Gomez moderates the project and may edit, hide, lock, or remove content; close
issues or pull requests; restrict interaction; or temporarily or permanently
block an account. The response depends on severity, repetition, risk to other
people, and whether constructive participation still appears possible.

Moderation decisions may be explained publicly when useful, but private details
and reporter identities will not be disclosed merely to justify an action.
