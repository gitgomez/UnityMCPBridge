# Support Policy

UnityMCPBridge is a personally maintained, needs-driven open-source fork. It is developed by Gomez for practical use, with substantial assistance from OpenAI Codex and GPT-based tools. Gomez reviews the resulting changes and remains responsible for the project. OpenAI does not sponsor, endorse, maintain, or support this fork.

## No Support Commitment

Use of UnityMCPBridge does not create a support or service relationship. There is no guaranteed:

- response or resolution time;
- maintenance or release schedule;
- compatibility with a particular Unity, Python, operating-system, MCP-client, or upstream version;
- investigation, implementation, or acceptance of an issue, feature request, or pull request; or
- continued development of any feature.

Issues and pull requests may be reviewed when they align with the maintainer's own needs, priorities, and available time. Community participation is welcome, but silence or closure does not imply a commitment to provide support.

Fork-specific support must not be expected from the upstream project or its maintainers.
